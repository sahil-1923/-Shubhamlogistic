Heavy and Oversized Cargo Transportation

ODC transportation requires more than a standard truck movement. Cargo dimensions, weight, vehicle selection, route access, permits and site conditions all need to be considered before the movement begins.

Shubham Logistics handles ODC movements for industrial machinery, heavy equipment, large machinery components, fabricated structures and project cargo.

We assess the movement requirements and coordinate the appropriate trailer, route planning, permits, escort support and delivery arrangements for the cargo.
