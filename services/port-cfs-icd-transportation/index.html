Transportation Pricing

Container Haulage Cost in India

Container haulage cost depends on the shipment route and transportation requirements rather than a fixed rate. The final quote is provided after reviewing the movement details.

The main factors that can affect container haulage pricing include:

  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Transportation distance
  • Container size and type
  • Loaded or empty container movement
  • Port, CFS or ICD movement requirements
  • Route and scheduling requirements
  • Loading and unloading requirements
