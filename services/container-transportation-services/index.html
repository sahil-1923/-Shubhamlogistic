Container Transport Services

Container Transport Services for Import & Export Cargo

Shubham Logistics Services manages road transportation for import and export container shipments. From port pickup to final delivery, each movement is coordinated according to the pickup location, destination and required schedule.

Our container transport service connects major ports with CFSs, ICDs, factories and warehouses through planned road movement.

Container Transportation Includes

  • Import and export container movement
  • Port-to-factory and port-to-warehouse transportation
  • Port-to-CFS and ICD transportation
  • Factory-to-port export transportation
  • 20ft and 40ft container transportation
  • GPS-enabled vehicle tracking
Container Movement Options

Container Transport for Every Major Movement

Choose the movement required for your import, export or inland container shipment. Each movement is planned according to the pickup point, destination and delivery schedule.

01
Port-to-Factory Container Transport
Move loaded import containers from the designated port directly to the factory.
02
Port-to-Warehouse Container Transport
Transport import containers from the port to the required warehouse or storage facility.
03
Port-to-CFS / ICD Transport
Move containers from the port to the designated Container Freight Station or Inland Container Depot.
04
Factory-to-Port Container Transport
Pick up loaded export containers from the factory and transport them to the required port.
05
Factory-to-CFS / ICD Transport
Move export containers from the factory to the designated CFS or ICD.
06
CFS / ICD-to-Factory Transport
Transport containers from a CFS or ICD directly to the designated factory.
