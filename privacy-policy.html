Privacy Policy

Learn how Shubham Logistic Services collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information shared through our website and logistics services.

Last Updated: 19 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, request a quotation, or contact us, we may collect your name, company name, email address, phone number, city or location, pickup and delivery details, cargo details, service requirements, and any other information you choose to provide.

We may also collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as browser and device information, pages visited, approximate location, and website interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, assess logistics requirements, provide logistics services, communicate with you, maintain business records, improve our website and customer experience, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Marketing Communications

We do not currently send promotional or marketing communications by WhatsApp, email, or SMS. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and follow applicable requirements.

4. Cookies and Google Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our website and to improve website performance, content, and user experience.

Google Analytics may collect information about website usage, including pages viewed, browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions with the website. We use this information in aggregated form for website analysis.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. To learn how Google uses information from websites and apps, please visit Google’s information page.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only where necessary with authorised employees of Shubham Logistic Services and service providers that help us operate our website and respond to enquiries, including Netlify Forms, email services, WhatsApp, and analytics providers. We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide services, maintain business and accounting records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymise, or securely retain it only where necessary.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have shared with us. You may also ask us to stop using your information for non-essential communications. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party services, including WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Google. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. Please review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website, legal requirements, or business practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
