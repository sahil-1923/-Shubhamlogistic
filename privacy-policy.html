Privacy Policy

Learn how Shubham Logistic Services collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information you share when using our website and logistics services.

Last Updated: 03 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, including our quote request and contact forms, we may collect information such as your name, company name, email address, phone number, city, cargo details, and any other information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, prepare quotations, coordinate logistics services, communicate service updates, and improve our customer support. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the information you provide against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Access to enquiry information is limited to authorized personnel responsible for providing our logistics services.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics, to improve website performance, analyze visitor traffic, and enhance user experience. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services, including Google Analytics and other services that help improve website functionality and user experience. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them if you use those services.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us by contacting us using the details below.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us.
